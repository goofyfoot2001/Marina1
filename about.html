Our Story

Three Centuries of River Town

New Bern Grand Marina sits at the exact point that shaped this town from the start — where the Neuse and Trent Rivers meet.

Est. 1710

Built on the point where the Neuse meets the Trent

New Bern was founded in 1710 at the exact confluence that still shapes the town today. New Bern Grand Marina occupies that same working waterfront, downtown, where the docks have always been.

Slips sit along the water just inside the Alfred A. Cunningham Bridge, with the marina office on the property next to a hotel and restaurant — so everything a visiting or resident boater needs is within a short walk of the water.

Next Door

Union Point Park, a five-minute walk from your slip

The park sits right at the confluence point, hosting New Bern's waterfront gatherings through the warm months — an easy walk from the marina for crew who want to stretch their legs downtown.

On Foot From the Dock

Downtown New Bern, without the drive

Craven Street's restaurants, shops, and galleries sit a short walk from the marina office — no shuttle, no car needed. Tie up and the historic district is already at your feet.
