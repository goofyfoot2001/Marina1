Why New Bern

A marina in the middle of it all

New Bern is a charming, historic waterfront town with a unique blend of European influence and Southern hospitality. Tie up at the Grand Marina and you're steps from restaurants, shops, and downtown New Bern — not a taxi ride away.

Know Before You Go

Alfred A. Cunningham Bridge

The marina sits just inside the Alfred A. Cunningham Bridge, with vertical clearance between 14 and 17 feet depending on water levels. Plan your arrival around the bridge schedule below.

Weekday Bridge Closures

Monday–Friday, excluding holidays:
6:30–8:30 AM and 4:30–6:00 PM
