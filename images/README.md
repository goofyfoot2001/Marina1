Put new photos here (logo, dock photos, etc.) and reference them in the HTML as `images/filename.jpg`.

Right now the logo and marina map on the live site are linked directly from the old nbgmyc.com site as placeholders. Once you have your own copies, add them to this folder and update the `<img src="...">` lines in index.html, marina-map.html, rates.html, and contact.html to point to `images/yourfile.jpg` instead.
